## 🔐 Why Go Paper?

Most people leave their $USDC on exchanges or hot wallets. That’s risky.  
In 2024, phishing attacks and wallet exploits are more common than ever.

Here’s why I switched to paper wallets:

- ✅ **Completely offline**: Your keys never touch the internet  
- ✅ **No app or plugin needed**  
- ✅ **Immune to browser hacks & phishing**  
- ✅ **100% self custody**

---

## 🛠️ How I Store My USDC

1. I generated my keys on an air-gapped computer using [usdcpaperwallet.com](https://usdcpaperwallet.com)  
2. Printed the public + private key using a laser printer  
3. Laminated the paper and sealed it in a fireproof, waterproof pouch  
4. Stored one copy in my safe, another in a secure vault
